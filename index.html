Web Developer & Game Enthusiast

🎓 What I Learned in Web Design Class

I have taken a web design class in highschool and I enjoyed creating a small website. Seeing how the URLs and functions work together has been fascinating. We ended up making a fully functional game in the class, which was pretty cool!

My game was a clicker game - one of the most rewarding projects I've worked on.

🎮 What the Game Did

It had a few buttons and images to click on. When you clicked a button, you would increment your money. Once you reached a certain amount, you could upgrade your clicking to earn more money per click.

Although it was small and silly, the coding aspect fascinated me. Seeing how the variables and button mechanics worked together really sparked my interest in programming.

🚀 My Vision for the Future

The way video games are coded and operate is what inspired me to become a video game designer. Seeing how creative work translates into code and receiving appreciation from the community is truly wholesome.

I am hoping to make a game that is loved by all and that could make me proud of how far I have come.
